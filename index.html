Historical Saga

The Long Road To Freedom

Key milestones that transformed a subcontinent's destiny and broke the shackles of colonialism.

1857

The First War of Independence

The Sepoy Mutiny and widespread resistance across northern India marks the first unified stand against British East India Company rule.

1885

Formation of Indian National Congress

Political organizing takes formal shape to advocate for rights, representation, and ultimate self-rule (Swaraj).

1930

Dandi Salt March & Civil Disobedience

Mahatma Gandhi leads a 240-mile march to the Arabian Sea, defying salt monopolies and inspiring millions worldwide.

1942

Quit India Movement

The clarion call "Do or Die" resonates across the country, demanding immediate and total British withdrawal.

1947

Midnight Tryst with Destiny

At the stroke of midnight on August 15, India steps forth into life and freedom as an independent sovereign democratic republic.
